Clinic Management
SYSTEM - TIP Manila

A Clinic Management System (CMS) is a digital tool designed to streamline healthcare tasks like appointment bookings and managing medical records. It helps reduce errors and save time by automating administrative processes. TIP CMS is specifically built for TIP Manila students, allowing them to schedule appointments and submit health data easily. The system improves efficiency and enhances the overall clinic experience for both students and staff.
